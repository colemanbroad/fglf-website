About your gift

Finley's Green Leap Forward became a registered 501(c)3 in 2024 — after a decade of growing the fund through the Northern Piedmont Community Foundation. Donations are now received directly and remain fully tax-deductible.

Every gift goes toward the planet. We make grants to a hand-picked group of organizations chosen for their direct, measurable impact — locally and globally. See exactly where the grants go →
