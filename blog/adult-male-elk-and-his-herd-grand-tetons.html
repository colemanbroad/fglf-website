Redwood Forest Foundation Tribute

Redwood Forest Foundation Posts Tribute To Finley Broaddus

Members of Finley’s extended family in California and Texas arranged for the Redwood Forest Foundation (RFFI) to plant 25 redwoods in her honor after a rare form of liver cancer claimed the teenager’s life. This tribute was a particularly fitting celebration of Elizabeth’s life, for she was an avid supporter of tree-planting projects.
