Listen Up!

Does any of us know it all? No, and it can be hard to keep up with news and new insights, even for matters we care about deeply like how to decrease our impact on the planet’s resources.

One relatively easy way to learn something new each week is to tune into radio programs or podcasts. We can listen while we cook dinner, walk the dog, ride the bus, fold the laundry: multitasking at its best!

Here’s a link to the podcast series “Good Stuff”. You can listen to individual stories, ranging from eliminating waste (who doesn’t want to do that?!), to palm oil, microbeads, and much more. Even better, you can subscribe to the series so that you won’t miss new episodes when they come along!

Do you know of other environmental podcasts? Don’t keep it a secret – please let us know!
