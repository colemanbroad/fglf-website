April 22, 2022

The fruits of our labor on Earth Day – $90,000 in grants announced today!

 
Thank you for making possible our biggest grant year ever! Finley’s Green Leap Forward is so pleased announce $15,000 grants to each of the following 6 wonderful organizations: The Greenbelt Movement, the Cacapon Institute, Cool Earth, Rainforest Trust, Eden Projects, and Reserva Youth Land Trust. This brings Finley’s giving total to $435,000 since she started her fund in 2014. We are so proud of you Finley, and so pleased to be able to do such great work for the planet in your honor. Thank you all for a very fruitful Earth Day!
