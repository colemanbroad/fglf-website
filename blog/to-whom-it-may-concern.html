To whom it may concern…

As Earth Day approaches, we are all looking for ways to make our voices heard. Quiet Sunday afternoons are the perfect time to write an old-fashioned letter.  Find a cause that you are passionate about and then the words will flow easily.  Maybe it is thanking your Governor for supporting The Clean Car Act or urging Amazon to use plastic free packaging.   Every action makes a difference. Companies and government officials are beholden to us so let them know where your priorities lie. 

Set yourself a goal like a postcard a week and it will quickly become a habit. So, dust off that pen & paper and get writing. 

For tips on writing an advocacy letter check this out:
