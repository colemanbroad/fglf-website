Put That Yard to Work: Be a Yardfarmer

Well, not everyone can be an official “yardfarmer”, but we can all get into the spirit of transforming a bit of our yards or decks into food-producing landscape.

If you want to get inspired to take it to a new level, check out Yardfarmers which is creating a reality TV show highlighting 20-somethings who dare to move back home and turn their parents’ coveted lawns into productive landscapes.

Watch the trailer – you can’t help but get inspired to at least plant a few lettuce or kale plants in your window boxes. But who knows, maybe there’s a true yardfarmer in your future.
