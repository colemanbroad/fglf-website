Save Time, Save Water, Save Your Clothes by Washing Them Less

Are you one of those people who throws your clothes and towels into the laundry hamper after a single use? Or equally shameful, have you raised your children to think that’s permissible? Every clothes washing cycle uses energy and water (which in turn use more water and energy respectively), AND cause a bit of damage to the fabric.

The Levi company has been particularly thoughtful and active in encouraging its customers to think before washing and to take steps like spot cleaning, washing with cold water, and line drying in order to reduce the environmental impact of the clothes we wear.

While jeans are known for being durable, most other outer garments as well as sheets and towels can wait much longer for a washing than you probably realize. Trust us on this one! And the best part, is you save yourself time and money in the process!
