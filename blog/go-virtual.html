Go Virtual

Paying your bills online can make a huge difference to the environment. If every US household received electronic statements, we could save 18.5 million trees, 2.2 billion tons of greenhouse gases and 1.7 billion pounds of solid waste per year.

It is also makes managing your finances effortless. You can see payment histories and budget future payments. No need to worry about overdue bills again!

Have you been hesitant to switch because of security concerns? Easy. Avoid public computers and Wi-Fi networks and make sure the Web page URL always starts with “https”. That “s” at the end signifies a secure connection.

Your finances will be streamlined in no time and your mailbox will thank you for it!
