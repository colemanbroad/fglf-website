Our mission

Finley's vision in three commitments.

Established in 2014 by Finley Broaddus to support actions that move us toward a vision of clean air, clean water, and a healthier, sustainable planet.

Reach Globally

Finley's Fund supports efforts around the world to make connections as we combat global environmental issues.

Engage Locally

Finley's Fund supports local environmental efforts to make our communities healthier, happier, and more sustainable.

Act Personally

Finley's Green Leap Forward engages individuals in personal action campaigns enabling us all to make a difference.
